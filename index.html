The Concern

An operator in the old sense of the word

Finnell Energy is a family-held oil and gas company in the traditional mold — built on leasehold, operated wells, and long-held mineral interests rather than speculation. We take positions we intend to keep, drill with our own names on the ticket, and stay hands-on from title room to tank battery.

Our work is deliberately unglamorous: careful acquisition, conservative development, and steady, well-run production. We answer our own phones, stand behind our agreements, and treat every lessor, surface owner, and working partner the way we would expect to be treated on our own ground.

The company is operated for the long term. That shapes everything — how we structure a deal, how we complete a well, and how we leave a location when the work is done.

The Practice

Four matters, handled thoroughly

i.

Acquisition & Title

Leasehold and mineral acquisitions conducted quietly and properly, with title work handled to the standard of a courthouse regular — no surprises carried into the drill site.

ii.

Drilling & Completion

Wells designed and supervised field-first: sound casing, careful cementing, and completions done once — and done right — on both oil and gas wells.

iii.

Production & Operations

Day-to-day operation of producing wells: dependable liftings, honest gauges, tidy wellsites, and relationships with surface owners that outlast the location.

iv.

Minerals & Royalties

Administration of mineral and royalty interests with clear division orders and payments that arrive when they should — the quiet courtesy that keeps country open for the next deal.

The Standard

I.

Patience over promotion

We would rather hold a good position for ten years than chase a headline this quarter. Deals are made to be kept, not flipped.

II.

Stewardship over extraction

The land, the water, and the lessor outlast any well. We operate as guests on other people's country — and behave accordingly.

III.

The operator's word, kept

In this business your name is your bond. Our agreements are plain-spoken, and we do what we said we would do.
